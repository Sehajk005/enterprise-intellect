REMOTE WORK GUIDELINES
Orbitra Technologies - People Operations
Effective 1 January 2026

1. ELIGIBILITY
Employees may work remotely once they have completed their probation period. Employees on
probation work from an Orbitra office unless their department head approves otherwise in writing.
Contractors work remotely only as set out in their services agreement.

2. HYBRID SCHEDULE
Roles that are suitable for hybrid work attend the office 3 days a week, on Tuesday, Wednesday and
Thursday. Monday and Friday are remote days. Department heads may set a different pattern for
teams with on-site obligations, such as the hardware lab.

3. CORE HOURS
Everyone is available between 11:00 and 16:00 India Standard Time, whether in the office or
remote. Outside core hours people may arrange their work flexibly with their team.

4. HOME OFFICE SUPPORT
Orbitra pays a one-time home office stipend of INR 15,000 to each eligible employee, claimed in
OrbitExpense with receipts. In addition, Orbitra reimburses internet costs of INR 1,000 per month
on submission of the monthly bill.
The stipend is not repeated if the employee changes teams, and it is not paid to contractors.

5. WORKING FROM ANOTHER COUNTRY
Employees may work from another country for up to 20 days in a calendar year. The request must be
sent to People Operations at least 30 days in advance because of tax, immigration and data
protection rules. Work from another country for longer than 20 days is not permitted.

6. EQUIPMENT AND SECURITY
Company laptops stay with the employee at home. The IT Acceptable Use policy applies fully while
working remotely. Equipment must be returned to IT within 10 days of the employee's last working day.

7. HEALTH AND SAFETY
Employees are responsible for a safe working area at home. Report any work-related injury at
home to People Operations the same day.

8. REVIEW
These guidelines are reviewed every year. Individual arrangements agreed before 1 January 2026
remain valid until their end date.
